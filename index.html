46-POINT INSPECTION

VEHICLE CHECK

Our inspection covers the key systems listed in your workshop job sheet.

01 — GENERAL CHECKS

Timing belt interval, seat belts, ABS/airbag warnings, AC/heater, wipers, horn, headlight alignment, doors and bonnet lubrication.

02 — ENGINE BAY

Cooling system, power steering, engine oil, oil/AC/air filters, battery, fuel filter, spark plugs and brake fluid.

03 — VEHICLE SYSTEMS

Gearbox and transfer oils, brake and fuel lines, suspension, CV joints, wheel bearings, clutch and front/rear brakes.

04 — FINISH & ROAD TEST

Oil refill, wheel-nut torque, oil re-check, road test, emission report, service indicator reset and tyre pressure.
